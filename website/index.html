About Kazakhstan's Space Legacy

🛰️

Space Heritage

Kazakhstan has been at the forefront of space exploration since the Soviet era, hosting the world's first and largest operational space launch facility.

🌍

Global Partner

Collaborating with international space agencies including NASA, ESA, Roscosmos, and private companies like SpaceX.

🔬

Innovation Hub

Developing indigenous satellite technology and fostering a new generation of space scientists and engineers.

Milestones & Achievements

1957

Sputnik 1, the world's first artificial satellite, launched from Baikonur Cosmodrome

1961

Yuri Gagarin's historic first human spaceflight departed from Baikonur

2006

KazSat-1, Kazakhstan's first national communications satellite, launched

2013

KazEOSat-1, Kazakhstan's Earth observation satellite system deployed

2021-Present

Continued partnership with international agencies for satellite launches and space research

Baikonur Cosmodrome

The World's First & Largest Space Launch Facility

Located in the desert steppes of Kazakhstan, Baikonur Cosmodrome has been the launch site for countless historic missions:

  • 🌟 First artificial satellite (Sputnik, 1957)
  • 👨‍🚀 First human in space (Yuri Gagarin, 1961)
  • 🛸 First woman in space (Valentina Tereshkova, 1963)
  • 🚀 Launch site for International Space Station missions
  • 📡 Over 1,500 spacecraft launches since 1957
